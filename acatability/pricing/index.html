Money questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account can try a pact free for 14 days. No card needed, and it never turns into a paid plan by itself.

Who pays in a pact?

One person. Everyone they invite joins free. Only the person who pays can invite.

What if the person paying stops?

Shared features pause for the pact until anyone in it subscribes. Everyone's journals and days are kept.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly and yearly plans renew until you cancel in your Apple ID settings, at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Prices are in UK pounds. The App Store shows your local price.
