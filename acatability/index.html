Journal with your best friend. Neither of you reads the other's.

A tiny daily journal for you and up to four friends. You each write privately. You just see that the others showed up today, and a small cat throws a party every time you do.

How it works

Two minutes in the evening. That's it.

  1. 1Write privately. Your journal is yours. No one else can read it, not even your friends.
  2. 2Share only what you choose. A few good things from your day, if you feel like it.
  3. 3See who showed up. Your friends know you checked in. The cat celebrates. He never nags.
