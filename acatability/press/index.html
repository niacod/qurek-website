Press kit

Writing about Acatability? Everything you need is here. Free to use.

In one line

Acatability is an iPhone app for small daily mental-health habits you keep with friends, with a cat who cheers you on.

In a paragraph

An iPhone app for small daily mental-health habits kept with friends. Each day you check in with a private journal, a few things you're grateful for and your journalling or meditation minutes. Friends in your pact see that you showed up, plus only what you choose to share. A cat cheers you on. Free on your own; one plan brings up to four friends in, and they join free.
