A 5-minute daily check-in for your mind

By the Acatability team · 18 October 2026 · 3 min read

You don't need an hour to look after your head. Five minutes a day is a good start. Here's a check-in you can do morning or night.

Minute 1: arrive

Put your phone face down after you open your notes. Take three slow breaths. Notice how you feel, without fixing it.

Minutes 2 and 3: write a line or two

What's on your mind? Write it plainly. "Tired. Big meeting tomorrow." is enough. Nobody is grading this.

Minute 4: three good things

Small counts. A warm drink. A text from a friend. Sunlight on the floor. Write them down.

Minute 5: one small intention

One thing for today or tomorrow. "Go outside at lunch." "Reply to Mum." Keep it doable.

Morning or night?

  • Morning: sets the tone. Good for the intention part.
  • Night: winds you down. Good for the good-things part.

Pick the one you'll actually do. You can switch later.

Short on time?

Do one minute. One line, one good thing. Showing up matters more than doing it perfectly.
