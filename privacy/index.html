Classy Pilates Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who we are

Classy Pilates is a pilates and wellness app operated by Qurek Limited, a company registered in England and Wales. In this policy, "we", "us", and "our" refer to Qurek Limited. If you have any questions, contact us at info@qurek.biz.

2. What data we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We store your email address to identify your account. If you enable push notifications, we store a device token to send you class updates. We do not collect payment information, location data, or any health data.

3. How we use your data

We use your email address solely to identify your account and provide access to the app. We use your push notification token only to send you notifications about new classes and studio updates, which you can turn off at any time in Settings.

4. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Google Sign-In — for account authentication (Google Privacy Policy applies)
  • Supabase — for secure database and account storage (data stored in EU)
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — for video content delivery

These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.

5. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or request deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days. Push notification tokens are deleted immediately when you turn off notifications.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are based in the UK or EEA, you have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You also have the right to object to processing and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@qurek.biz.

7. Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies or third-party tracking or analytics tools. We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers.

8. Children

Classy Pilates is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. Continued use after changes are published means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please email us at info@qurek.biz.
